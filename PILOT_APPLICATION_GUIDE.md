# Apex Air - Pilot Application Guide

## 📝 How to Apply to Join Apex Air

Follow this guide to submit your application to join our virtual airline.

---

## ✅ Prerequisites

Before applying, ensure you have:

- ✅ **VATSIM Account** - Active and in good standing
- ✅ **Minimum Flight Hours** - 10+ hours on VATSIM (preferred)
- ✅ **VATSIM Rating** - None required
- ✅ **Email Address** - Valid, working email

---

## 🚀 Application Process

### Step 1: Create Pilot Account

1. Visit: `http://www.apexair.eu/`
2. Scroll to "My Account" section
3. Click "Register here" 
4. Fill in your information:
   - **Full Name** - Your name
   - **Email Address** - Valid email
   - **VATSIM ID** - Your VATSIM ID number
   - **Password** - Strong password (8+ chars, letters, numbers)
   - **Confirm Password** - Re-enter password
5. Click "Create Account"

✅ Your pilot account is now created!

### Step 2: Complete Application

After account creation:

1. You'll see **"Apply to Join VA"** button
2. Click the button to open application
3. Your info auto-fills (name, email, VATSIM ID)

### Step 3: Fill Flight Experience

1. **Total Flight Hours** - Enter your VATSIM flight hours
2. **VATSIM Rating** - Select from dropdown:
   - S1 - Suspended Pilot (Basic)
   - S2 - Suspended Pilot (Intermediate)
   - S3 - Suspended Pilot (Advanced)
   - C1 - Commercial Pilot
   - C3 - Airline Transport Pilot
   - I1 - Instructor

### Step 4: Select Aircraft Preferences

Check the aircraft types you prefer to fly:
- ✓ Airbus A320
- ✓ Airbus A321
- ✓ Boeing 737
- ✓ Boeing 777

*You can fly other aircraft after approval*

### Step 5: Availability

Select when you're available to fly:
- **Weekdays** - Monday-Friday
- **Weekends** - Saturday-Sunday
- **Both** - Any time

### Step 6: Agreement

Check these boxes:
- ☑ I agree to follow Apex Air Code of Conduct and VATSIM regulations
- ☑ I agree to the terms and conditions

### Step 7: Optional Info

In the text area, you can add:
- Why you want to join
- Your flight simulation experience
- Your preferred routes
- Any special interests

*This is optional but recommended!*

### Step 8: Submit

Click **"Submit Application"**

⏳ Your application is now under review by Apex Air admins!

## 🔐 Account Login

### Logging Back In

If you close your browser:

1. Go to "My Account" section
2. Click "Login here"
3. Enter **email OR name** + password
4. Click "Login"

Your account is saved, so you can reapply later if needed.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### How many flight hours do I need?

Minimum 10 hours recommended. You can apply with less, but approval chances increase with experience.

### Can I change my application?

Log back in and submit a new application. Only the latest application is reviewed. You can apply again after rejection.

### What if I'm rejected?

You can:
- Build more experience
- Improve your VATSIM rating
- Apply again after 30 days
- Contact recruitment@apexair.eu

### Do I need to own all aircraft?

No! We have fleet aircraft available. You just need compatible add-ons for the types you want to fly.

### Can I change my availability later?

Yes! Log into your profile and submit a request with updated availability.

### How do I update my flight hours?

After approval, admins will track your hours. You don't need to update them yourself.

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

### Can't Create Account

**Error: "Email already registered"**
- Use different email or login with existing account
- Reset password if you forgot it

**Error: "Password too weak"**
- Password must be 8+ characters
- Include uppercase and numbers
- Example: `MyPassword123`

**Error: "VATSIM ID invalid"**
- VATSIM ID must be numeric
- Check it's correct from your VATSIM profile

### Application Won't Submit

**Problem: Form won't submit**
- Ensure all fields are filled
- Check agreement boxes are checked
- Try refreshing page
- Check browser console (F12)

**Problem: Red error message**
- Read error carefully
- Fix required fields
- Try again

### Can't Login

**Forgot Password:**
- Contact: recruitment@apexair.net
- Include your email and name

**Can't Remember Username:**
- Use your registered email
- Or use your full name
