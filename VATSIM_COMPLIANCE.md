## VATSIM Code of Conduct Compliance

Apex Air operates in full compliance with the [VATSIM Code of Conduct](https://vatsim.net/documents/code-of-conduct). All pilots and staff must adhere to the following:

### Core Principles

✅ **Professional Conduct**
- Maintain professional behavior at all times on the VATSIM network
- Use proper callsigns and identifiers
- Follow realistic flight procedures

✅ **Respect and Courtesy**
- Treat all pilots, controllers, and staff with respect
- Avoid disruptive behavior
- Follow ATC instructions without argument

✅ **Safety and Realism**
- Maintain realistic flight operations
- Follow established flight rules (VFR/IFR procedures)
- Report violations or misconduct

## Pilot Requirements

All Apex Air pilots must meet the following criteria:

### Minimum Qualifications
- **VATSIM Account:** Active and in good standing
- **Flight Experience:** Minimum 50 hours on VATSIM network
- **Rating:** S1 or higher (Suspended Pilot or above)
- **Age:** 13+ years old (VATSIM requirement)
- **Language:** Fluent English communication

### Procedural Requirements
- Complete Apex Air pilot orientation and safety briefing
- Familiarize with standard operating procedures (SOPs)
- Understand aircraft systems and performance data
- Agree to follow realistic departure/approach procedures
- Acknowledge Apex Air Code of Conduct

### Conduct Standards
- No vulgar language or harassment
- No unauthorized flight plans
- No reckless flying (aerobatics, low-level buzzing in inappropriate areas)
- Maintain professional radio phraseology
- Report squawk codes and flight information accurately

## Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

### Pre-Flight
1. File flight plan 30 minutes before departure
2. Verify aircraft performance and weight/balance
3. Check NOTAM (Notices to Airmen)
4. Plan alternate airports
5. Request ATC clearance

### In-Flight
1. Maintain proper callsign: AAX[FlightNumber]
2. Monitor and follow ATC instructions
3. Maintain appropriate flight levels
4. Report position at designated waypoints
5. Request landing clearance 10-15 minutes before destination

### Post-Flight
1. File pilot report (PIREP) if notifying of significant conditions
2. File flight completion records
3. Update logbook hours
4. Report any incidents or issues

## Violation Reporting and Discipline

Apex Air enforces strict standards to maintain network integrity.

### Reportable Violations
- Reckless flying (aerobatics, excessive speed)
- Disruptive behavior toward ATC or other pilots
- Unauthorized deviation from filed flight plan
- Violation of VATSIM rules
- Use of exploits or unauthorized software

### Disciplinary Actions
1. **First Offense:** Written warning
2. **Second Offense:** Probationary period (2 weeks)
3. **Third Offense:** Suspension from airline (1 month)
4. **Serious Violations:** Immediate dismissal

## ATC Interaction Best Practices

### Proper Radio Procedures
✅ **DO:**
- Use professional, clear phraseology
- Request clearance from ATC before filing
- Follow assigned vectors and altitudes
- Acknowledge ATC instructions
- Report reaching assigned altitude

❌ **DON'T:**
- Interrupt or cut off ATC
- Argue with ATC decisions
- Use improper language
- Ignore ATC instructions
- File unauthorized flight plans

### Communication Example
```
Pilot:   "Boston Ground, AAX101 with information Delta, request pushback"
ATC:     "AAX101, Boston Ground, pushback approved, wind 270 at 8, QNH 29.92"
Pilot:   "Pushback approved, AAX101"
```

## VATSIM Network Rules

### General Rules
1. Maintain realistic speed, altitude, and flight characteristics
2. Follow instrument flight procedures when using instruments
3. Don't perform prohibited maneuvers (barrel rolls, loops, inverted flight)
4. Maintain separation from other aircraft
5. Don't use "X-Plane damage" or ultra-realistic damage modes that would prevent landing

### Restricted Airspace
- Respect restricted military airspace
- Follow published procedures
- Request clearance through proper channels
- Don't enter without authorization

### Server Rules
- Keep connection stable (minimal disconnects)
- Don't reconnect repeatedly in short timeframe
- Use realistic aircraft models only
- Don't use modified or unrealistic liveries
